One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude — meditate with Lucia on the balcony at Jason's house in Chapter 1, after the Down to Earth mission, before the chapter ends. Everything else on the list can be earned in free roam after the story. Full warning in the roadmap below.

Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10, one playthrough, no online trophies and no mission replay. Play the story first, then the side strands, then clean up the open world, then the money — the checklist only becomes viewable after the story, under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. Sources: PowerPyx and KeenGamer roadmaps.

Missables: only Positive Mental Attitude is possibly missable (see the banner). Story trophies cannot be missed — finishing the story earns them all. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so play clean. Side quest givers who go quiet during the story come back later.
